Every member can check their own usage; owners can review organization spending and set limits.

## Check your usage

Open **Settings → Personal settings → Your usage**.
Check credits used, remaining allowance or **No limit**, and the reset date for the selected organization.

Usage resets monthly.
Go uses calendar months in UTC; Pro follows the subscription's monthly anniversary, including when the subscription is billed annually.

## Set a monthly spending limit

As an organization owner:

1. Open **Organization settings → Plan and credits**.
2. Under **Monthly spending limit**, choose **Set a limit**.
3. Enter the number of credits and click **Save limit**.

Choose **No limit** and save to remove the cap.
A zero limit blocks new platform-paid agent requests.
The same limit applies separately to every member, including owners; it is not a total organization budget.

Changes apply immediately to usage already recorded in the current period.
Limits do not reserve credits from the shared balance, and work already running may finish above the limit.
BYOK requests are exempt from this upfront limit check, although platform operations can still consume shared credits.
Raising a limit does not add credits.

## Review organization spending

Open **Organization settings → Activity → Credits** to see total and daily spending, and credits by person.
Use **Usage period** to view the current or an earlier period and the person selector to filter the daily chart.

Upgrading to Pro starts a new usage period.
Earlier charges remain in their original period.
Reports show settled charges; recent work may take a moment to appear.

The **Activity** tab counts completed work such as requests, saved edits and applied updates.
Activity counts are not credit charges; manual document edits are free.
**Unattributed usage** has no individual member and is included in organization totals.

## Review credit history

In **Plan and credits**, click **View credit history** beside **Shared credit balance**.
Filter by activity type and use **Previous** or **Next** to browse charges, credits added, refunds and expiry.

Charges for the same conversation, person and source are combined within each page, with a turn count.
Totals include only turns on that page; a conversation may appear on another page.
Grants, refunds, expiry and charges without a conversation remain separate.

Billing access does not grant access to private conversations or documents.
Restricted or deleted conversations can appear as **Unavailable item** with their charges still recorded.

## When you cannot continue

- **Limit reached:** ask an owner to raise or remove the limit, or wait until the displayed reset date.
- **Shared credits depleted:** an owner can [buy credits or enable auto reload](/plans-and-pricing/buy-credits-and-auto-reload).
- **Usage failed to load:** reload the page, or use **Retry** if shown.

See [How credits work](/plans-and-pricing/how-credits-work) for expiry, shared balances and what consumes credits.
