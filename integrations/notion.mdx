Connect your Notion workspace so Falconer can search and reason over the pages your team has already written.

## What's synced

Pages from the workspace you connect. You choose which pages to sync or import.

## What you can do

- Search every connected page.
- Ask Falcon questions about your Notion content.
- Keep Notion knowledge in sync with the rest of your documentation.

## Connect Notion

1. Navigate to **Integrations** from the bottom-left menu.
2. Click **Connect** next to Notion.
3. Authorize Falconer and select at least one page to share.

If your token expires or permissions change, use **Reconnect** to re-authorize.

You must select at least one page when connecting. If no pages are selected, Falconer has nothing to sync and will prompt you to reconnect.

## Visibility

Synced Notion pages are visible to everyone in your Falconer organization. Notion page permissions do not carry over into Falconer, so only share pages your whole organization should be able to see.

## Disconnect Notion

Open the menu on the Notion card on the **Integrations** page and click **Disconnect**.
