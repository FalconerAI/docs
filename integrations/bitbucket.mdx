Connect Bitbucket so Falconer can answer questions about the code your team keeps in Bitbucket.

## What's synced

Repositories, code files, and merged pull requests updated in the last 90 days, with their comments.
Falconer syncs every repository in the Bitbucket workspaces your account can access.

## Who can see Bitbucket content

Synced Bitbucket code and pull requests are visible to everyone in your Falconer organization.
Bitbucket repository permissions are not applied, so content from private repositories is visible to all members too.

## What you can do

- Ask questions about your codebase and get code-aware answers.
- Find Bitbucket code and pull requests in search alongside your docs and other sources.

## Connect Bitbucket

1. Navigate to **Integrations** from the bottom-left menu.
2. Click **Connect** next to Bitbucket.
3. Authorize Falconer to access your Bitbucket account.

Falconer begins syncing as soon as you authorize.

Connecting Bitbucket requires owner permission to your Falconer organization.

## Reconnect or disconnect

Once Bitbucket is connected, use **Reconnect** to authorize again, for example after your access changes.
Use **Disconnect** to remove the connection.
Disconnecting also removes the synced Bitbucket data from Falconer.
