Connect Jira so Falconer can reason about the work your team is tracking, right alongside your code and docs.

## What's synced

Projects, issues and their comments, releases, boards, and sprints from the Jira projects you select.
Comments restricted to a Jira role or group are not synced.

## What you can do

- Search Jira issues alongside code and documentation.
- Ask Falcon about the status of projects, releases, and sprints.
- Reference Jira issues, projects, boards, and sprints in your documents with @ mentions.

## Connect Jira

1. Navigate to **Integrations** from the bottom-left menu.
2. Click **Connect** next to Jira.
3. Authorize Falconer to access your Atlassian account.
4. In **Configure Jira**, choose a Jira site and click **Continue**.
5. Select the projects to sync and click **Save and sync**.

Use **Manage** at any time to add projects or to disconnect Jira.
To stop syncing a project you already synced, disconnect Jira and connect it again.
If your token expires or permissions change, use **Reconnect** to re-authorize.

Jira and Confluence are connected separately, each from its own card on the **Integrations** page.

Connecting Jira requires owner permission to your Falconer organization.

## Visibility

Synced Jira content is visible to everyone in your Falconer organization.
Jira project permissions and issue security levels are not applied in Falconer, so only sync projects your whole organization should be able to see.
