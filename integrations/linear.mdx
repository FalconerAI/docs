Connect Linear so Falconer can reason about the work your team is shipping, right alongside your code and docs.

## What's synced

Issues, projects, milestones, and Linear documents.

## What you can do

- Search tasks alongside code and documentation.
- Generate changelogs from completed work.
- [Create Linear issues from Slack.](/use-cases/automate-workflows/create-linear-issues-from-falconer)

## Connect Linear

1. Navigate to **Integrations** from the bottom-left menu.
2. Click **Connect** next to Linear.
3. Authorize Falconer to access your Linear workspace.

Falconer begins syncing immediately. If your token expires or permissions change, use **Reconnect** to re-authorize.

Connecting Linear requires owner permission to your Falconer organization.
