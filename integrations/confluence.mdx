Connect Confluence so the documentation your team has built up over the years becomes searchable alongside your code, tasks, and conversations.

## What's synced

Pages from the Confluence spaces you select.

## What you can do

- Search across all of your connected Confluence spaces.
- Ask Falcon about documentation and runbooks.
- Keep Confluence content in sync so you find the latest version, not stale links.

## Connect Confluence

1. Navigate to **Integrations** from the bottom-left menu.
2. Click **Connect** next to Confluence.
3. Authorize Falconer to access your Confluence account.
4. In the **Select Confluence spaces** dialog, choose your Confluence site and click **Continue** if you are asked to.
5. Select the spaces you want to sync, then click **Save selection**.

To change which spaces are synced, choose **Manage** on the Confluence card. Use **Reconnect** to authorize again, or click **Disconnect** in the **Select Confluence spaces** dialog to remove the connection.

Confluence is available on Custom plans. Contact [support@falconer.com](mailto:support@falconer.com) to learn more.
