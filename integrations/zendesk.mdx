Connect Zendesk so your support knowledge is searchable alongside your internal documentation.

## What's synced

Help center articles. Falconer indexes these articles so they show up in search. Tickets are not indexed. Falconer reads them from Zendesk when you need them, for example in a table synced to Zendesk tickets or in an automation that runs when a ticket is created or updated.

## What you can do

- Search help center articles alongside your docs.
- Keep a Falconer table in sync with Zendesk tickets.
- Start an automation when a ticket is created or updated, or when an article is published.

Connected articles are searchable alongside the rest of your documentation.

## Connect Zendesk

1. Navigate to **Integrations** from the bottom-left menu.
2. Click **Connect** next to Zendesk.
3. Enter your **Zendesk subdomain** (for `https://yourcompany.zendesk.com`, enter `yourcompany`) and the **Email** you use to sign in to Zendesk.
4. Optionally, enter a **Zendesk API token**. You only need one if you are not connecting with OAuth.
5. Click **Connect**.

To remove the connection, open the Zendesk dialog again and click **Disconnect**.

Zendesk is available on Custom plans. Contact [support@falconer.com](mailto:support@falconer.com) to learn more.
