Connect Zendesk so your support knowledge is searchable alongside your internal documentation.

## What's synced

Help Center articles. Tickets are not synced or indexed.

## What you can do

- Search Help Center articles alongside the rest of your documentation.
- Ask Falcon questions that draw on your published support articles.
- Pull Zendesk tickets into a table that stays in sync. See [Sync a table with work](/sync-a-table-with-work).

## Connect Zendesk

1. Navigate to **Integrations** from the bottom-left menu.
2. Click **Connect** next to Zendesk.
3. Enter your Zendesk subdomain and authorize Falconer.

Zendesk is available on Custom plans. Contact [support@falconer.com](mailto:support@falconer.com) to learn more.
