Connect the tools your team already uses so Falconer can search, write, and keep documentation up to date across your stack.

Each integration takes a few clicks to set up. For the general connection flow, prerequisites, and what each button means, see [Connect sources](/set-up/connect-sources).

You can also ask Falcon to connect a missing source while you are working in a conversation. It shows a connection card, takes you through authorization, and brings you back to your request. See [Connect integrations with Falcon](/connect-integrations-with-falcon).

## Other connections

The pages below cover common sources, not every service you can use. From the Integrations page, you can add supported remote MCP servers from a searchable list or enter a custom server URL. These connect Falcon to the tools that server offers. You can also connect [PostgreSQL or MySQL](/connect-a-database) with access limited to the tables and columns you choose. Availability depends on your organization’s settings and the provider.
