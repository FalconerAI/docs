Connect Slab so the posts in your team's knowledge base are searchable alongside the rest of your documentation.

## What's synced

Posts from your Slab workspace, organized by topic.
A post that belongs to several topics appears under the first one.
Draft and private posts are synced too, but only their owner can see them in Falconer.
Archived posts are not synced.
A post archived or deleted after it was synced stays in Falconer until you disconnect Slab.

## What you can do

- Search your Slab posts alongside the rest of your documentation.
- Ask Falcon questions about your Slab content and get answers that cite the original posts.
- Browse Slab content by topic when Falcon explores your sources.

## Connect Slab

1. In Slab, open the [Team Settings > Developer](https://slab.com/settings/developer) page and generate an API token.
2. In Falconer, navigate to **Integrations** from the bottom-left menu.
3. Click **Connect** next to Slab.
4. Paste the token into **Slab API token** and click **Connect**.

Falconer checks the token with Slab and begins syncing immediately.

Generating a Slab API token requires a Slab Business or Enterprise plan.
Connecting Slab requires owner permission to your Falconer organization.

## Manage the connection

Once connected, click **Manage** on the Slab card to see the connection status and when Slab was last synced.
Use **Update token** to replace the API token, or **Disconnect** to remove Slab and its synced content from Falconer.

If your API token expires or is revoked, the Slab card shows a warning and a **Reconnect** button.
Click **Reconnect**, enter a new token, and click **Connect** to resume syncing.
