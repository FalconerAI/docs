Falconer and general-purpose AI assistants like Claude or ChatGPT are complementary tools. Here's how to think about when to use each.

## Use Falconer for company knowledge

Falconer is purpose-built for your organization. It has full context on your docs, tasks, code, and teammates -- so you get better answers without having to explain background every time.

**Use Falconer to:**

- Ask questions about your company's docs, code, or processes
- Reference specific teammates, tasks, or files in your answer
- Write or update internal documentation
- Turn a conversation into a shareable doc in one click

> **Note:** When the question is about your company, Falconer will provide answers using your context, even with shorter prompts.

## Use an AI chatbot

Tools like Claude or ChatGPT are well suited for tasks that go beyond your company's knowledge base.

**Use them for:**

- More up-to-date information from the internet
- Memory across many past conversations
- Broad, open-ended tasks that don't depend on internal context
