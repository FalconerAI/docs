Every conversation you have with the Falcon agent is saved as a session.
Use the **Sessions** page to pick up where you left off, keep important conversations close at hand, and clean up the ones you no longer need.

## Open your sessions

There are two ways to get to your past conversations:

- In the left sidebar, click the **Sessions** icon to open the Sessions page.
- On the Falconer home page, click the history icon in the top right to see your **Recent chats**, then click **See all in Sessions** to open the full list.

Click any session to reopen the conversation and keep chatting.
Sessions shared with you open with their full history, and you can reply to continue the conversation.
To start a fresh conversation from the Sessions page, click **New chat**.

## Browse and search

The Sessions page shows how many sessions you have under its title.
Sessions you've pinned appear under **Pinned**, and everything else appears under **Recent**.

To find a specific conversation, type in the **Find sessions...** search box.
Falconer matches your text against the title and the start of the opening message of the sessions already loaded on the page.
Pinned sessions are always loaded.
To find an older session, clear the search box and scroll down to load more, then search again.

## Manage a session

Click the **More** icon on a session's row to see its actions:

- **Pin** keeps the session at the top of the page under **Pinned**. Click **Unpin** to move it back to **Recent**.
- **Rename** lets you edit the session's title in place. Press **Enter** to save or **Esc** to cancel.
- **Share** lets everyone in your organization read and reply to the session, and copies its link to your clipboard. See [Collaborate in conversations](/collaborate-in-conversations).
- **Delete** removes the session. Falconer asks you to confirm before deleting it.

Pinning only changes your own list, so pinning a session never affects how it appears for anyone else.

## Sessions shared with you

A session a teammate shares with you directly appears in your list right away.
A session shared with your whole organization appears once you open it.
Shared sessions show a **Shared** label. Hover over it to see who shared it.

Their menu offers only **Pin** and **Leave**.
**Leave** removes the session from your list without deleting it for its owner.
