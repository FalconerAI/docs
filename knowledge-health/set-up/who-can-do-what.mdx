See who can run, schedule, and pay for Knowledge Health scans.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Members | Open Knowledge Health, run a scan, change the schedule, and stop a run. |
| Owners | Add credits, turn on **Auto reload**, and get the **Knowledge Health paused** alert. |
| Owners | Connect and configure sources. Some tools also need an admin on their side to approve Falconer; see that integration’s page. |

## What Knowledge Health can read

Falconer reads only the sources, repositories, folders, and channels you approve when you connect them. To limit what Knowledge Health audits, limit what you connect. See [Change sources later](/knowledge-health/set-up/connect-sources#change-sources-later).

For organization roles, see [Invite teammates and manage permissions](/set-up/invite-teammates-and-manage-permissions).
