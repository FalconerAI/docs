See which Knowledge Health notifications you get, who gets them, and how to turn them off.

## Knowledge Health notifications

| Notification | Who gets it | Default channels |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Knowledge Health report ready** | The person who started the first scan on a newly connected source, when its report is ready | Inbox, email, and Slack |
| **Knowledge Health report follow-ups** | The same person, starting the day after the first report: up to three emails, no more than one a day | Email |
| **Knowledge Health paused** | Owners, when a scan pauses for credits | Inbox and email |
| **Knowledge Health report shared with you** | People a teammate shares the report with | Inbox and email |

## Change your notifications

Later scans don’t send a notification when they finish, and you aren’t notified when you’re made a finding’s owner.

Open **Settings**, choose **Personal settings**, then **Notifications**. Turn each notification on or off for **Inbox**, **Email**, and **Slack**. The report notifications are under **Document lifecycle**, and **Knowledge Health paused** is under **Billing**. To stop the follow-up emails, use the unsubscribe link in any of them.
