Turn a space into a docs site, choose where you write, and pick its address.

## Prerequisites

- **Public docs are enabled for your organization.**
  Contact Falconer if the option isn't available.
- **You're an organization owner,** or you have edit access to the space and an owner will publish.
- **A space to publish.**
  A docs site is set up on a [space](/organize/spaces), not on a regular folder, and each space can have one site.

## Create a site with a new space

1. In the space switcher, click **New space** and give the space a name.
2. Turn on **Set up public docsite**.
3. To write in a repository, also turn on **Sync with GitHub** and pick the repository.
   Pages then come from the repository instead of the starter pages.
4. Click **Create space**.

## Add a site to an existing space

1. Open the space and choose **Set up public docs** from its **More actions** menu.
2. Choose where you write docs:

   | Option | What it means |
   | --- | --- |
   | **Web editor** | Edit documents visually in Falconer. Always on. |
   | **GitHub repository** | Also sync the space with a repository, so engineers can edit the same pages in Git. See [Set up two-way sync](/two-way-sync/set-up-two-way-sync). |

3. Finish the setup.

## What a new site starts with

- Three starter pages: **Welcome to your docs site**, **Publish your first build**, and **Set a custom domain**.
  They're removed automatically if you later connect a repository and haven't edited them.
- A starter favicon and logos in the **Files** tab.
- A default navbar button and social links that point at Falconer.
  Replace them with your own in [Navbar](/public-docs/customize-your-site#navbar) and [Footer](/public-docs/customize-your-site#footer).
- An address at `your-space-name.falconer.site`.

The space now has two tabs in the left nav.
**Docs** is the site's navigation, and **Files** is the folder of files behind it.
See [Docs view and Files view](/public-docs/docs-and-files-views).

## Change the address

The site card at the top of the space shows its address.
Owners can change it:

1. Click the pencil next to the address, labelled **Edit address**.
2. Enter a new **Docs site path** and click **Save this address**.

The address is a single label of 1 to 63 lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens, with no hyphen at the start or end, and it has to be unique across Falconer.
A few labels such as `www`, `app`, `api`, `docs`, and `admin` are reserved.

When you change the address of a live site, the new address starts serving first and the old one is removed.
Falconer then rebuilds and republishes so links, the sitemap, and `llms.txt` use the new address.
Older builds show **Built for outdated subdomain** and keep the old address in their links until you build again.
