Point a subdomain at Falconer, or serve the docs under a path on your main site.

Open **Site configuration** and choose **Custom domain**.
You need to be an organization owner who can manage billing.

A custom domain is a paid add-on billed monthly per site in credits, 15,000 credits per month.
Setting up and verifying the domain is free; billing starts when you enable it.

## Choose how to serve the site

| Option | Example | How traffic reaches Falconer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Subdomain** | `docs.example.com` | A CNAME record in your DNS |
| **Path on your domain** | `example.com/docs` | A rule in your own web server or hosting that forwards the path to Falconer |

An apex domain such as `example.com` isn't supported on its own.
Use a subdomain, or serve the docs under a path.

## Subdomain

1. In your DNS provider, create a CNAME record from your subdomain to `cname.falconer.com`.
   The CNAME both routes traffic and proves you control the domain, so no separate verification record is needed.
2. In **Custom domain**, choose **Subdomain**, enter the domain, and click **Check and confirm**.
   If the record hasn't propagated yet, Falconer tells you to add it first.
   New DNS records can take a few minutes to resolve.
3. Wait for the status to change from **Verifying** to **Ready to enable**.
   Falconer issues a certificate automatically and keeps checking; click **Check now** to check immediately.
4. Click **Continue** to enable the domain.
   The status moves through **Going live** to **Active**.

HTTPS is handled for you.
The certificate is issued and renewed automatically.

## Path on your domain

![The Custom domain section showing an active path domain with the forwarding rule and proxy snippets](/images/public-docs-custom-domain.png)

1. In **Custom domain**, choose **Path on your domain**, enter the address such as `example.com/docs`, and click **Continue**.
2. Add a forwarding rule to the site that serves `example.com`.
   Falconer shows the exact rule, with ready-made snippets for **Vercel**, **Next.js**, **Cloudflare**, and **Nginx**.
   The rule forwards `/docs` and `/docs/*` to your site's Falconer address, keeps the path, and sends the Falconer host name, the visitor's IP address in `X-Forwarded-For`, query strings, and cookies if the site has a password.
   Don't cache the docs path on your side.
3. Publish the site, then click **Check now**.
   Falconer requests a marker page through your domain and confirms it reaches the live build.
   The status changes from **Waiting for proxy** to **Address verified**.
4. Click **Continue** to enable the domain.

If the check fails, the message says what went wrong: a redirect, a 404, a wrong host header, your own page being served instead of the docs, or an HTTPS problem.

## Domain status

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Verifying** or **Waiting for proxy** | Falconer is waiting for DNS or your forwarding rule |
| **Ready to enable** | Verified. Enable it to start serving and billing. |
| **Going live** | Rolling out to every edge location |
| **Active** | Serving the site |
| **Disabled** | Turned off. The setup is kept and can be restored. |
| **Failed** | Something went wrong. Check the message and try again. |

## Disable or remove

- **Disable** stops serving the site on the domain and stops billing.
  Everything stays configured, and **Restore** brings it back without a new charge if the paid period hasn't ended.
- **Remove** deletes the domain setup.
  Disable an active domain first.
  To switch to a different domain, remove the current one and start again.
