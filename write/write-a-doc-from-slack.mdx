Turn any Slack conversation into a structured Falconer document.

## Prerequisite

Slack is connected to your Falconer org.

## Steps

1. Mention `@Falcon` in a Slack thread with a request like "write a new doc for this"
2. The bot analyzes the conversation thread, extracts context, processes any images, and creates a formatted document
3. Falcon replies in the thread with a preview of the doc and an **Edit document** button. Click it to open the doc in Falconer and refine it.

## Tips

- **Provide clear instructions:** Say what you need, such as new documentation, a summary of the discussion, or a record of decisions.
- **Check who can see it:** If the thread is in a public channel, everyone in your organization can open and edit the new doc. Docs from private channels or DMs stay restricted until you share them.
- **Leverage updates:** Falcon can also update existing documents based on later Slack conversations.
