Bring docs, code, and conversations into one place your team and agents can search.

</Card>

## Public docs

Publish customer-facing docs from the same source your team writes in.

</Card>

Keep a Falconer space and a GitHub repository in sync, in both directions.

Docs update when related code changes, automatically or after your review.

Connect GitHub, Linear, Jira, Slack, Notion, Google Docs, and Confluence to bring context into your docs.
